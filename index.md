# Hello, I am Hasanur

For a long time, I overthought how to start something and confused preparation with progress.

I am going to change that.

These are principles I will stick by as I continue to learn, build and grow:

- **Thinking about doing the thing is not doing the thing. Just do the thing.** 
- **You won't wake up as a better version of yourself tomorrow. Start today.** 
- **There is no perfect path to learning. You will waste time doing something wrong. But next time you will 
    be better at it.**
- **No work is dead work. It all compounds.** 
- **The difference between the novice and the master is that the master has failed more times than the novice has tried.**
